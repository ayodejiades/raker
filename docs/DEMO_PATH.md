# Demo path

1. **Raker finds class-action settlements you qualify for, files the claim by email, and tracks the reply live.** — go to `/`
2. **This is the live dashboard, backed by Convex with settlements discovered by a Firecrawl crawl of public settlement trackers.** — (narration only)
3. **Each row is a real open settlement with its provider, deadline, and eligibility summary, synced live with no polling.** — (narration only)
4. **Raker provisions its own dedicated AgentMail claims inbox, which sends the claim and receives the administrator's reply.** — (narration only)
5. **One click on File claim sends the email from Raker's inbox, and the claim appears below the settlement with its live status.** — (narration only)
6. **When the administrator replies, the webhook flips the status to confirmed or needs info. Discover, file, and track in one place.** — (narration only)

Everything not listed above is stubbed, hardcoded, or deleted. That is the correct prioritisation under a deadline, not technical debt.
